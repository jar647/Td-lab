What the score means

The 0–100 score is a transparent prioritization heuristic, not a prediction. It weighs defensive TDs allowed per game (35%), defensive red-zone opportunities allowed (20%), player red-zone share (25%), and player red-zone opportunities per game (20%). Inputs are capped for scoring, and smaller samples are discounted. The same formula across positions is a temporary baseline and requires validation.

American odds imply a break-even probability. A true betting edge requires an independently estimated, calibrated touchdown probability. Past TD rates alone are not sufficient.
