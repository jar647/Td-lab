About the data & matchup research

Schedules, records, venues and bout results come from ESPN. Main-card and prelim labels appear only when the provider explicitly supplies them; unpublished card positions remain unverified. Times use your device’s time zone and may change.

Fight breakdowns explain the statistical lean, opposing evidence and possible paths for each fighter. Available partial profiles remain visible; a lean requires every mathematically necessary career metric; unused percentages remain marked unavailable. Striking and grappling metrics appear only when supplied by the event provider or the authenticated fighter research cache. Missing data stays unavailable. The experimental model uses those metrics independently of odds; its estimates are uncalibrated and do not establish betting value. Simulation intervals measure sampling error, not predictive accuracy.

Moneyline calculations use the prices you enter. The no-vig baseline removes the two-price margin; it is a market calculation, not an independent prediction. Prices are saved on this device when storage is available.

Top pick selects one supported winner, standard total or aligned win method using a fixed, unvalidated evidence rule. No odds input is required. It does not establish betting value or calibrated accuracy; unsupported or already-started fights do not receive a new pre-fight pick.

Round totals use completed fights with matching scheduled lengths and usable finish times. Method research compares actual recorded wins and losses by KO/TKO, submission and decision. These are historical samples, not calibrated probabilities for the current fight. Entered prop prices show break-even rates only; a heavy favorite does not imply a finish or a better-value prop.
