What was actually recorded?

Performance

Hit rate = wins ÷ (wins + losses). Pushes, voids, pending and missing results are excluded. Units and ROI require a real price recorded before the game; an unpriced research pick is not a verified profitable bet. Records sharing a game or player are related, not independent trials.

Does a higher score help?

Compare within the same sport, board, model version, market and side. A 90/100 score is not a 90% win probability. Different lines and overlapping player/game exposure can change hit rates. No score-based model changes are made automatically.

Tracking rules and coverage

Coverage: NBA Best Props, game-first best single/top-three/default ticket, top-five first basket; NFL game analyst top-three/default ticket and TD position leaders; UFC top fight pick and supported predicted winner. Cancelled fixtures are voided when confirmed. DNP requires participation evidence; missing data stays unresolved. First basket uses first made field goal and requires starting. Sportsbook settlement rules can differ.

Capture runs after successful source-refresh workflows and on a 10-minute fallback schedule. GitHub scheduling and source delays can leave gaps; games missed before tipoff stay unrecorded. Tracking starts with actual pregame captures, not reconstructed historical winners.
