Important model limits

Each run resamples real paired historical game-score errors, preserving the observed relationship between margin and total. The sportsbook's lines and prices do not influence the simulated scores. Historical scoring patterns and 100,000 draws do not establish predictive calibration or a verified edge. No live sportsbook odds, official inactives, confirmed starters, or game-time weather are assumed. Research-only estimates can be wrong, especially with changing rosters, limited samples, or stale feeds.
