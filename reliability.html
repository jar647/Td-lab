When should I trust a betting edge?

A matchup score out of 100 is not a win probability. A simulation percentage is not a verified sportsbook edge until independent, same-version forward results show that it is calibrated and the quoted price is real and executable.

Historical backtests, 100,000 Monte Carlo draws, and green GitHub Actions do not substitute for those checks. An unverified market remains RESEARCH ONLY, not a forced recommendation.

The report is read-only and cannot place bets or modify model results. Historical odds in the repository may reflect closing lines, not executable pregame prices.
